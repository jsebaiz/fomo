The rules

01What counts as a member
Someone counts when they've created a fomo account, submitted that username and fomo email through your chapter link, and been approved on the roster. A submitted username is a claim, not a member.
02Your roster size is locked at registration
The active count you entered is the denominator for every stop. Only the fomo team can change it, and only with proof — a nationals listing or an exec board confirmation. This exists so no chapter clears 100% by declaring itself eight people deep.
03Stops stack, and each one pays once
Clearing 90% pays stop two on top of the $500 you already received — you're not being re-paid, you're being topped up. A stop already paid can't be earned again.
04Dropping back takes nothing away
If members leave and your percentage falls, you keep everything already paid. Stops above your current level just pause until you're back over the line. We don't claw back.
05The chapter gets paid, not a person
Money goes to the chapter account, arranged through the treasurer or president named on your registration. If your chapter can't take an ACH transfer, we'll cut a check to the house. We won't send a payout to a personal Venmo.
06Timing
A stop is confirmed within 3 business days of being hit, and paid within 7 business days of confirmation. Crates ship inside 10 business days of stop-one confirmation.
07Duplicates and dead accounts
Before a stop is confirmed we remove accounts that share a device, email alias or payment method with another account, and accounts that never opened the app after signing up.
08Referrals pay on results
A referral pays when the chapter you sent reaches stop one, not when they register. They have to name you on step 3 of their registration — we can't backfill a referral from memory.
09One chapter per school, per organization
Two registrations for the same letters at the same school are merged into whichever came first, and the counts combined. Colonies and interest groups are eligible; alumni chapters are not.
10Paperwork over $600
Once a chapter passes $600 from us in a calendar year, US tax rules require a W-9 before we can send more. Stop one alone stays under that line; stop two crosses it.
11Sororities, same program
Every number here applies identically to sororities, co-ed and professional chapters. Nothing about the stops, the crate or the referral bonus changes.
12This is a program, not a contract
fomo can change these amounts or end the program with notice on this page. Any stop you've already hit is honored at the amount posted when you hit it — changes only apply forward.

Questions we actually get

Are these photos of the real merch?
No — everything in the crate is a design mockup, not a product shot. The letters, the fomo mark and the placement are what you'll get, and picking your chapter repaints the gear in your school's colours. Real photos replace these once the first run ships.
Do pledges count toward our roster?
Yes, as long as they're on the roster number you registered with. Use the figure you'd give your nationals. What you can't do is count pledges in the numerator and leave them out of the denominator.
How fast do chapters usually hit 80%?
The ones who get there almost always do it in the first two weeks, off a single push — chapter GroupMe, one meeting announcement, link in the bio. Chapters that drip it out over a month tend to stall in the fifties.
Can the money go to an individual instead?
No. Payouts go to the chapter account only. A few thousand dollars landing in a student's personal account is a problem for your nationals, your university and that student's taxes.
Does our leaderboard rank change what we get paid?
No. The leaderboard ranks by raw members, so big chapters dominate it. This roadmap is a percentage of your own roster, so a 40-person house and a 200-person house have the same shot at every stop.
