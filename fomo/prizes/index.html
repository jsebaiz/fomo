The rules

How the money actually works.

Written out so nobody has to guess, and so no chapter ever argues about a payout after the fact. If something here isn’t clear, that’s our problem — email us before you start and we’ll fix the wording.

01

What counts as a member

Someone counts when they’ve created a fomo account, submitted that username and fomo email through your chapter link, and been approved on the roster. A submitted username is a claim, not a member — pending rows don’t move your bar.

02

Your roster size is locked at registration

The active count you entered is the denominator for every stop. Only the fomo team can change it, and only with proof — a nationals listing or an exec board confirmation. This exists so no chapter clears 100% by declaring itself eight people deep.

03

Stops stack, and each one pays once

Clearing 90% pays stop two on top of the $500 you already received — you’re not being re-paid, you’re being topped up. A stop already paid can’t be earned again, including after a reset.

04

Dropping back takes nothing away

If members leave and your percentage falls, you keep everything already paid. Stops above your current level just pause until you’re back over the line. We don’t claw back.

05

The chapter gets paid, not a person

Money goes to the chapter account, arranged through the treasurer or president named on your registration. If your chapter can’t take an ACH transfer, we’ll cut a check to the house. We won’t send a payout to a personal Venmo.

06

Timing

A stop is confirmed by the fomo team within 3 business days of being hit, and paid within 7 business days of confirmation. Crates ship inside 10 business days of stop-one confirmation. You’ll get an email at each step.

07

Duplicates and dead accounts

Before a stop is confirmed we remove accounts that share a device, email alias or payment method with another account, and accounts that never opened the app after signing up. This is the only part of the process that can move your number down without anyone leaving.

08

Referrals pay on results

A referral pays when the chapter you sent reaches stop one, not when they register. They have to name you on step 3 of their registration — we can’t backfill a referral from memory. Ten per chapter per semester.

09

One chapter per school, per organization

Two registrations for the same letters at the same school are merged into whichever came first, and the member counts are combined. Colonies and interest groups are eligible; alumni chapters are not.

10

Paperwork over $600

Once a chapter passes $600 from us in a calendar year, US tax rules require a W-9 before we can send more. Stop one alone stays under that line; stop two crosses it. We’ll send the form — it’s one page and it goes in the chapter’s name.

11

Sororities, same program

Every number on this page applies identically to sororities, co-ed and professional chapters. Nothing about the stops, the crate or the referral bonus changes.

12

This is a program, not a contract

fomo can change these amounts or end the program with notice on this page. Any stop you’ve already hit is honored at the amount posted when you hit it — changes only ever apply forward.

Questions we actually get

Before you ask.

Are these photos of the real merch?
No — everything in the crate is shown as a design mockup, not a product shot. The letters, the fomo mark and the placement are what you’ll get, and picking your chapter above repaints the gear in your school’s colours. The exact garment cut and print finish get locked when your crate goes into production, and real photos replace these once the first run ships.
Do pledges count toward our roster?
Yes, as long as they’re on the roster number you registered with. Use the figure you’d give your nationals — actives plus the current pledge class. What you can’t do is count pledges in the numerator and leave them out of the denominator.
Our roster changed mid-semester. Now what?
Email us with the new number and something that backs it up. We’ll update the denominator and recalculate. If the change pushes you over a threshold you hadn’t hit, the stop pays normally. If it pushes you under one you’d already been paid for, rule 04 applies — you keep the money.
How fast do chapters usually hit 80%?
The ones who get there almost always do it in the first two weeks, off a single push — chapter GroupMe, one meeting announcement, link in the bio. Chapters that drip it out over a month tend to stall in the fifties. That’s the entire reason the speed bonus exists.
Can the money go to an individual instead?
No. Payouts go to the chapter account only. It protects you as much as us — a few thousand dollars landing in a student’s personal account is a problem for your nationals, your university and that student’s taxes.
What if someone deletes their fomo account?
Your count drops by one. If that takes you under a threshold you haven’t been paid for yet, the stop waits until you’re back over. If you’d already been paid, nothing happens.
Does the leaderboard rank change what we get paid?
No. The leaderboard is bragging rights — it ranks by raw members brought in, so big chapters dominate it. The roadmap on this page is a percentage of your own roster, so a 40-person house and a 200-person house have exactly the same shot at every stop.
