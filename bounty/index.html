04 · the fine print

How the money actually works.

01

The chapter gets paid, not a person

Bounty money goes to the chapter account through the treasurer or president on your registration — the same route as your onboarding money. We won't send a bounty payout to a personal Venmo.

02

Repeatable unless the brief says otherwise

Most bounties can be run again in a later month. The ones that can't say so in the brief. Re-sending the same work twice doesn't pay twice.

03

Real people, real footage

Everyone on camera agrees to be there and is 18 or older. No one is filmed who hasn't said yes. Stock footage, reposts and anything you didn't shoot are not submissions.

04

Contacts have to be real and warm

An intro counts when the person actually replies. A list of handles counts when they're real accounts at your school. Scraped lists and cold-pasted names don't count and we can tell.

05

Nothing that breaks your own rules

Don't run anything that puts you sideways with your nationals, your university or the law. No alcohol in frame with anyone underage. If a bounty would cause you a problem, skip it — there are twenty-one others.

06

We can use what you send

Submitting gives fomo the right to use the footage and photos in campus marketing, with your chapter credited. Tell us at submission if something is off-limits and we'll respect it.

07

Paperwork over $600

Past $600 to one chapter in a calendar year, US tax rules need a W-9 before we can send more. It's one page and it goes in the chapter's name. Bounty and onboarding money count toward the same total.

08

This is a program, not a contract

fomo can add bounties, retire them or change what they pay, with notice on this page. Anything already approved is paid at the amount posted when you ran it.

Bounties are open to chapters already onboarded to fomo campus. fomo is a trading app and all trading involves risk, including the risk of losing money — nothing on this page is a promise of profit or financial advice, and no bounty requires anyone to trade or deposit. Payouts shown are the current posted amounts.
