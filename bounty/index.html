The rules

Sending work in, and getting paid
  • Upload straight from the bounty. Keep the raw files until it is paid.
  • We check it in 3 business days and pay within 7 days of approval.
  • Money lands in the fomo account you named above, through your treasurer or president, never a personal Venmo.
  • Payouts move with the campus. A bounty whose value depends on where it happens pays up to 24% either side of base; the rest pay the same everywhere.
  • Live bounties pay a boost for the days that event runs. The rate you see when you submit is the rate we pay.
  • Most bounties repeat in a later month. The ones that do not say so in the brief.
  • Everyone on camera agreed to be there and is 18 or older. Nothing you did not shoot.
  • An intro counts when the person replies. Scraped lists do not count.
  • Nothing that puts you sideways with your nationals, your university or the law.
  • Submitting lets fomo use the footage in campus marketing, your chapter credited. Tell us if something is off-limits.
  • Past $600 to one chapter in a calendar year we need a W-9 before sending more.
  • Amounts can change with notice here. Anything already approved pays at the amount posted when you ran it.
