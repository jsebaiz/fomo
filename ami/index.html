Here’s the whole thing

Four steps. That is it.

No deposit, no quota, no minimum. Start with your group chat and add more whenever you feel like it.

Step 01

Apply to host

Two minutes. Your handle, your school, and an honest sentence about who your people actually are.

Step 02

Get your codes

We send a batch of invite codes tied to you. Every person who uses one is counted to your name automatically.

Step 03

Bring your people

Send codes to the people you would actually text on a Friday — your group chat, your floor, your house, your team.

Step 04

Get paid

We match every code, confirm who went live, and send one payment. Venmo, Zelle or bank — your call. Track it below.

When it lands

We count at the end of each month and pay within seven days. One payment, not a drip, and never a gift card.

How it lands

Venmo, Zelle or bank transfer — whichever you already use. We ask for that once you are approved, never on this page.

Past $600

Over $600 in a calendar year we need a W-9 before sending more. US tax law, not our rule. It does not affect your financial aid.
