Here’s the whole thing

Four steps. That is it.

No deposit, no quota, no minimum. You can start with ten people and add more whenever you want.

Step 01

Apply to host

Two minutes. Your handle, your city, and an honest sentence about who your circle actually is.

Step 02

Get your codes

We send a batch of invite codes tied to you. Every person who uses one is counted to your name automatically.

Step 03

Bring your circle

Send codes to the people you would actually text on a Friday. They join, they go live once, they are on the map.

Step 04

Get paid

We match every code to its Ami, confirm who went live, and pay you in a single transfer. Track it below.
